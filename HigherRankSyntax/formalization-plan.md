# Initiality of `Ctx.model`: strategy and formalization plan

Goal: for every `M : HrS.Structure`, exactly one `HrS.Morphism Ctx.model M`,
with the morphism an honest `def` (no choice in any definition).

Marks: `[probed]` a Lean probe compiles; `[open]` not yet attempted.

---

## 1. The pairing law (done)

`HrS.Structure` includes the naturality of pairing, the standard
category-with-families law:

```lean
pair_comp : ∀ {Γ Δ Ξ : Ob} {a : Ty Γ} (σ : Sub Δ Γ) (t : Tm Δ (substTy a σ)) (θ : Sub Ξ Δ),
  comp (pair σ t) θ = pair (comp σ θ) (substTy_comp a σ θ ▸ substTm t θ)
```

Without it a morphism out of `Ctx.model` cannot preserve composition, and every
coherence of reindexing below needs it. `Ctx.model` satisfies it from
`homEquiv_naturality` (`Ob.pair_comp`, `Ty₁.pair_comp`) `[proved]`.

With it, lifting a substitution through an extension is determined: it is `σ`
after the projection, paired with the generic term. So `HrS.Structure` has no
lifting field; `Bind_subst` and `lam_subst` state that pair directly, and
`HrS.Morphism` has no `onSub_lift`. `HrS.Morphism` relates structures in two
universes, so initiality covers models in every universe. `[proved]`

---

## 2. The strategy

A **partial interpretation** of raw syntax (Streicher), valued in Mathlib's
`Part` (a domain proposition with a getter), computed **relative to a semantic
environment** that assigns to each slot its semantic binding telescope,
boundary and term (Lumsdaine–Mörtberg). Semantic boundaries carry **data tags**
(sort / element / equation). The typing assumptions that a total function could
not justify during its own definition become `Part.assert`s in the domain.

Then, all on raw syntax: renaming is an equality, reindexing and hereditary
substitution are **lax** (defined on the left implies defined and equal on the
right). **Totality and soundness** are one mutual induction on the seven
judgements, quantified over all environments typed by the ambient. The morphism
is `Part.get` of the totality proof, lifted through the quotients of `Ctx`.
**Uniqueness** is separate from the interpretation: `Ctx.model` is generated by
its operations, and where two morphisms agree is closed under them.

---

## 3. The difficulties and how each is met

### 3.1 A typed interpretation of raw syntax is not a function

Two clauses need, inside the definition, a semantic equation that is a
soundness fact about the function being defined:

```text
Wf_bd.eq   heq : Eq_bd (Ξ ⋈ Θ) (boundaryOf l) (boundaryOf r)
           M.IdElement ⟦l⟧ ⟦r⟧ needs both at ONE M.El s; heq only gives Eq_e S S'

Wf_s.cons  equation : Eq_e (Ξ ⋈ bind) l r       at an equational slot
           the filler must inhabit M.IdX ⟦l⟧ ⟦r⟧, so ⟦l⟧ = ⟦r⟧ is needed
```

Soundness cannot be interleaved by stratifying on the ambient, because
`Eq_e.congr` over `Ξ` has a premise over `Ξ ⋈ Θ` for an arbitrary `Θ`.

**Solution: partial interpretation.** Undefined on ill-typed syntax; each
needed equation is asserted, not proved:

```text
eqClause ⟨of S, tl⟩ ⟨of S', tr⟩ = Part.assert (S' = S) fun h => some (eqEl S tl (h ▸ tr))
equational entry               = Part.assert (tl = tr) fun h => some (h ▸ IdX_refl tl)
```

This is Streicher's method, used by every initiality proof examined (Hofmann
§3.5, Castellan–Clairambault–Dybjer, Brunerie–de Boer, Lumsdaine–Mörtberg,
Uemura). Mathlib's `Part` is exactly Brunerie–de Boer's `Partial`
(`isDefined : Prop`, getter), and `Part.assert` is their `assume`: no
decidability of semantic equality is needed, and the value is recovered by
`Part.get` of a proof, with no choice `[probed]`. Functionality of the graph
(`v ∈ ⟦e⟧`) is `Part.mem_unique`; inversion is `Part.mem_bind_iff` /
`Part.mem_assert_iff`.

### 3.2 Models are not contextual, and slots have higher rank

A model has no way to read a variable off an object, so the interpretation is
relative to an **environment** `E : Env M Δ Γ` assigning a value to every slot
of `Δ` over the object `Γ` — Lumsdaine–Mörtberg's `environment`, the Initiality
Project's `Tm^V`, Uemura's `term^γ`. At higher rank a slot's value must carry
its semantic binding telescope:

```text
Value Γ α = (binding chain over Γ of arity α, its decoration, a tagged boundary
             at its end, a term of that boundary's type OVER THE END of the chain)
```

The value is **open**: its term lives over the end of its binding chain rather
than being closed under `Bind`. Then `⟦ap x args⟧ = (E x).atom[s]` for the section
`s` that the arguments build, and the interpretation of expressions never uses
`unlam`; `unlam` appears once, in the generic value of an entry, when an
environment is extended.

The syntactic ambient enters only through a predicate `Env.Typed Ξ E` (each
slot's value lies in the interpretation of its syntactic binding and
declaration) — UniMath's `typed_environment`. Being a predicate, it involves no
induction–recursion.

**Semantic telescopes without induction–recursion.** A chain carrying each
entry's binding telescope in its type would be induction–recursion. Instead: a
bare `Chain` of types, `Chain.Bind` by recursion on it, then a decoration
`Tele` *indexed by* the bare chain whose constructor records
`hA : A = Chain.Bind c_b B` `[probed]`. Its arity index is the syntactic arity,
so slot lookup follows `dTel.declaration` by `C.split`.

### 3.3 "Sort or element" cannot be read off a semantic type

In an arbitrary model `U` and `El` are neither disjoint nor injective, so the
dispatch between `IdSort` and `IdElement` in an equation `eq l r` must use
**data tags** on semantic boundaries:

```lean
inductive SemBd (M) (Γ : M.Ob) | sort | of (S : M.Tm Γ (M.U Γ))
  | eqSort (l r : M.Tm Γ (M.U Γ)) | eqEl (S) (l r : M.Tm Γ (M.El S))
```

This is the role annotations play in Hofmann (his exercise E3.40 shows the
method breaks without them). Elsewhere no annotation is needed: expressions are
spine-form `ap x args`, so they **synthesize** their boundary from the head's
environment value (the bidirectional split of the Initiality Project). A tag
mismatch is simply `Part.none`.

### 3.4 Substitution computes: the hereditary substitution lemma

`Subst.act` performs β while substituting, and the syntax has no β-rule; a
model has only `lam`/`unlam` and strict reindexing. So the semantic
substitution lemma must absorb β.

**Shape: the semantic twin of `Ambient.Filling` / `substitutionAt`.** A relation
`SemFilling Ω E' E` marks each slot of the environment as renamed (same value) or
filled (by the interpretation of an expression, at an arity below the bound
`Ω`). One lemma, `interp_fill`, then covers instantiation and every `Subst.act`.
It is proved like `substitutionAt`: well-founded recursion on `Ω` along
`Carrier.Sub`, structural recursion on the expression inside. This is the
lexicographic scheme of Keller–Altenkirch for hereditary substitution. Stating
it directly along `Subst.act.induct` (the principle Lean generates for
`Subst.act`) fails at the real call site: after the induction the head's arity
is an unfolded `Subtype.mk`, and the equation lemmas no longer match `[probed]`.

**The β case.** With open values, the head-in-the-block case is one pure-model
lemma: *reading a slot's value along the section built from a filling gives the
interpretation of the filler* — pairing laws plus `pair_comp`, no syntax
`[probed]`. The induction hypothesis at the head's smaller arity then finishes
the case. η (`lam_unlam`) enters through "unlam commutes with reindexing" and
through the identity lemma for `Expr.η`, which gives the identity substitution,
renamings and the generic term.

No initiality proof in the literature covers hereditary substitution at
arbitrary rank; Keller–Altenkirch prove its soundness only for the βη term
model of simple types, and Fiore–Mahmoud assert functoriality by definition.
This lemma is new work, but it follows their proof pattern case for case, and
it mirrors `Typing/SubstitutionLemma.lean`.

Statement shape, following Brunerie–de Boer and de Boer's thesis: lax,

```text
v ∈ ⟦e⟧ at (E₁ agreeing with ⟦σ⟧)   →   v ∈ ⟦σ ⋆ e⟧ at E₂
```

because substitution can make an asserted equation true. The environment must
be **uncertified** in the statement (no typedness premise), or the induction
becomes circular: typedness of the substituted ambient needs the lemma for the
ambient's own entries, which are not subterms of `e`.

### 3.5 Soundness: one induction, a strong form for equality

For every environment typed by `Ξ`:

```text
Wf_e  Ξ e      → ⟦e⟧ defined, and its boundary lies in ⟦boundaryOf e⟧
Eq_e  Ξ e e'   → ∃ v, v ∈ ⟦e⟧ ∧ v ∈ ⟦e'⟧
Eq_bd Ξ β β'   → ⟦β⟧ = ⟦β'⟧
Wf_s  Ξ Θ σ    → for every d ∈ ⟦Θ⟧, the walk of σ along d is defined
Eq_s  Ξ Θ σ θ  → for every d ∈ ⟦Θ⟧, the two walks agree
Wf_bd Ξ Θ β    → for every d ∈ ⟦Θ⟧, ⟦β⟧ over the extension is defined
Wf_t  Ξ Θ      → ⟦Θ⟧ defined
```

This is UniMath's `is_interpretable` shape. For fillings, state it in
accumulator form — for every partial section `g` whose reindexed decoration lies
in `⟦Θ⟧`, the walk of `σ` from `g` is defined — so the instantiated tail of
`Wf_s.cons` and `Eq_s.cons` needs no separate bridge lemma, only functoriality of
reindexing decorations and `lift ∘ pair = pair`. The same form gives `onSub`, as
a walk from the map into the empty object. The strong form for `Eq_e` (a
common value, rather than "equal if both defined") is forced by `Eq_e.congr`,
which has no well-formedness premises for its two expressions: hereditary
substitution can erase an ill-typed argument, so definedness of `σ ⋆ e` does
not give definedness of `e`. Cases: `refl`, `symm`, `trans` are immediate
because equality is interpreted as equality; `hyp` is `IdSort_reflect` /
`IdElement_reflect` at the slot's value after `unlam`, reindexed along the
arguments' section; `congr` is the induction hypothesis over `Ξ ⋈ Θ` at the
environment extended by `⟦σ⟧`, pushed through the substitution lemma.

### 3.6 Descent through the quotients of `Ctx`

Lumsdaine–Mörtberg report quotients as their main pain, because the dependent
eliminator does not compute in UniMath. Lean's `Quotient.lift` and
`Quotient.hrecOn` compute, so a single data-level `Quotient.hrecOn` over objects
(`envOf`) reduces by `rfl` on representatives `[probed]`; `onTy`, `onTm`,
`onSub` are `Part.get` of totality under `Quotient.lift`, and respect is
soundness plus functionality. The remaining cost is heterogeneous equality
through the three-level tower behind `Tm₁`.

### 3.7 Uniqueness

Uniqueness needs no interpretation at all. Two independent facts:

- **`Ctx.model` is generated** (pure `Ctx`): any family of predicates on its four
  sorts closed under the operations holds everywhere. By induction on raw
  syntax: every context is the empty one extended entry by entry, every type is
  `Bind` iterated over its binding at one atom, and every term is a slot's
  generic term `unlam`ped and reindexed along the pairing of its arguments — the
  identity principle `act_ap_eta` (`MonadLaws.lean`, already proved), i.e. the
  decomposition of Fiore–Mahmoud.
- **The equalizer of two morphisms is closed** (pure `M`, no quotients): where
  `F` and `G` agree is closed under every operation, by the preservation fields
  alone; equational terms by `IdX_irrelevant`.

Hence any two morphisms agree `[probed: the reduction to these two facts]`.
Alternatives: membership of `F`'s values in the interpretation, closed by
`Part.mem_unique` (Brunerie–de Boer's route); or naturality of the
interpretation along morphisms plus "interpreting into `Ctx.model` is the
identity" (the Initiality Project's route, whose second half Lumsdaine–Mörtberg
left unfinished). Generation requires decomposing a context at a middle slot
(prefix, entry, suffix); the existing `Ctx` files do this only for the last
entry, so that decomposition is the real work of the pass.

The `rfl` computation rules of `Ctx.model` removed as unused in the cleanup
(`Bind_mk`, `lam_mk`, `El_ofFill`, `Ob.pair_mk`, `Tm₁.subst_ofFill`, …) are
exactly what the generation induction consumes; they come back here, one line
each.

### 3.8 Computability

The final maps are `(…).get h` with `h` a proof; `Part.get` reduces through
`bind` and `assert`; tag dispatch matches on `SemBd` constructors, which are
data; transports go along Prop equations; `Quotient.hrecOn` is computable. No
definition uses choice. (`#print axioms` still lists `Classical.choice`, which
enters through Mathlib proof terms already present in the carrier; an
axiom-clean development would be a separate audit.)

## 4. Alternatives considered and rejected

| approach | why not |
|---|---|
| a total typed function on raw syntax | fails at the two clauses of §3.1 |
| inductive graph relation + recomputation from an `∃`-certificate (the previous plan; Bove–Capretta, Braga method) | every graph constructor is one `Part` clause anyway; measured on the application and equation fragments at 3–10× the code (≈140 vs 22 lines, ≈50 vs 4), plus an inversion lemma per family, since `cases` fails dependent elimination at concrete arities `[probed]` |
| Type-valued copies of the judgements | would duplicate `Weakening.lean` and `SubstitutionLemma.lean` |
| intrinsic quotient-inductive syntax (Altenkirch–Kaposi, Kaposi–Kovács–Altenkirch) | Lean has no QIITs, and it would replace the existing development |
| abstract initiality (Uemura, Isaev, Cartmell, Voevodsky's C-systems, Palmgren–Vickers) | constructs *an* initial model; does not show that this concrete `Ctx.model` is initial |
| explicit-substitution syntax (Castellan–Clairambault–Dybjer) | would make the substitution lemma trivial, but this syntax computes substitution |

## 5. Central definitions

All in pure `M`, except where syntax is named. Signatures are as probed; the
names are proposals.

```lean
inductive Chain (M) : M.Ob → C.Arity → M.Ob → Type u
  | nil  {Γ} : Chain Γ 1 Γ
  | cons {Γ α Δ Γ'} (A : M.Ty Γ) (c : Chain (M.extend Γ A) Δ Γ') : Chain Γ (C.single α ⋈ Δ) Γ'
def Chain.Bind : Chain Γ Ω Γ' → M.Ty Γ' → M.Ty Γ          -- with Chain.lam, Chain.unlam

inductive SemBd (M) (Γ : M.Ob)   -- sort | of S | eqSort l r | eqEl S l r ; SemBd.ty, SemBd.subst

inductive Tele (M) : Chain M Γ Ω Γ' → Type u   -- decoration indexed by the bare chain
  | nil  : Tele .nil
  | cons (d_b : Tele c_b) (B : SemBd M Γ_b) (A : M.Ty Γ) (hA : A = c_b.Bind B.ty)
         (d : Tele c) : Tele (.cons A c)

structure Value (M) (Γ : M.Ob) (α : C.Arity)   -- chain, decoration, boundary, term
structure Env (M) (Δ : C.Arity) (Γ : M.Ob) where slot : ∀ ⦃α⦄, Δ ∋ α → Value M Γ α
abbrev Atom (Γ) := Σ b : SemBd M Γ, M.Tm Γ b.ty            -- what an expression synthesizes

def walk : Env M Δ Γ → Tele M c → M.Sub Γ Γ_c → (argument interpretation) → Part (M.Sub Γ Γ')
def interp : Env M Δ Γ → Expr Δ → Part (Atom M Γ)          -- structural on Expr [probed]
def interpBd : Env M Δ Γ → Bd Δ → Part (SemBd M Γ)         -- eqClause with its assert
def interpTele : Env M Δ Γ → dTel Δ Ω → Part (Σ Γ' (c : Chain M Γ Ω Γ'), Tele M c)
def Env.Typed (Ξ : Ambient Δ) (E : Env M Δ Γ) : Prop
def SemFilling (Ω) (E' : Env M Δ' Γ) (E : Env M Δ Γ) : Prop -- slots renamed, or filled below Ω
def envOf (X : Ctx.Ob) : Σ Γ : M.Ob, Env M X.arity Γ       -- the one data-level hrecOn [probed]
```

`walk` checks a slot's arguments against its decoration and accumulates the
section with `pair`; it recurses on the semantic decoration, not on the
instantiated syntactic tail, which fails structural recursion `[probed]`.

Each pass: declarations proposed to the user before writing, then a report.

| pass | content | depends on | lines (est.) | risk |

| 3 | renaming (=), reindexing (≤), typedness of extended environments, the slot lemma, weakening along ambient renamings; twin of `Typing/Weakening.lean` | 2 | 600 | medium |
| 4 | `SemFilling` and `interp_fill` (well-founded on the block arity, like `substitutionAt`); its boundary, telescope and walk forms; the η lemma; twin of `Typing/SubstitutionLemma.lean` | 3 | 450–1300 | **high**: extending `SemFilling` under binders; may need several passes |
| 5 | totality and soundness: the mutual theorem of §3.5, `Eq_t` soundness, totality at `Ambient.Wf` | 4 | 500 | medium: the `Wf_s`/`Eq_s` tail, the one case that is not a congruence |
| 6 | descent and the 22 morphism fields: `envOf`, `onOb`, `onTy`, `onTm`, `onSub` | 5 | 1000 | medium-high: heterogeneous equality through `Tm₁`'s quotient tower; `onSub` over the renamed ambient |
| 7 | uniqueness (§3.7): `Ctx.model` is generated; the equalizer of two morphisms is closed; the restored computation rules of `Ctx.model` | 2 (independent of 3–6) | 700–1300 | medium-high: decomposing a context at a middle slot |
| 8 | `def initial (M : HrS.Structure.{v}) : Unique (HrS.Morphism Ctx.model M)` | 6, 7 | 30 | low |

Total ≈ 5–6.7k lines (the three independent designs estimated 5.2k, 5.4k and
6.7k), calibrated on `Typing/Weakening.lean` (429 lines) and
`Typing/SubstitutionLemma.lean` (1122), and on the Agda interpretation-to-
initiality chain (≈3.8k) and the Coq development (≈7k with quotients). These are
estimates, not measurements.

Pass 7 depends only on the definitions, not on passes 3–6, so it can run in
parallel with the substitution lemma.

**Tactic friction seen in every probe.** Objects computed by a chain's end
(`Chain.last`) and arities that come out as unfolded `Subtype.mk` defeat `rw`;
equation lemmas must be stated at the elaborated arities, and some steps needed
`erw`. Keep `interp`, `walk` and `interpTele` irreducible behind their
membership lemmas, both for this and for elaboration cost.

Pass 1 gates everything: a wrong shape for chains, decorations or environments
is paid for in every later clause, so its interface is settled against probes
of passes 2 and 4 before it is written out. Pass 4 carries the risk.

## 7. Decisions taken

1. `pair_comp` added to `HrS.Structure` (§1).
2. The lifting fields dropped; lifting is the pair of `σ` after the projection
   with the generic term.
3. `HrS.Morphism` generalized over two universes.
4. The `rfl` computation rules of `Ctx.model` are restored in pass 7.
